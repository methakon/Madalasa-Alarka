## 1. Parties and eligible use

These terms would govern the relationship between **SWARNA SEKHAR DHAR (Individual, India)** and a parent/guardian using Madalasa–Alarka. Initial intended territory is **India**, for children **0–17 inclusive**. The user must hold legally sufficient authority to supervise/configure that exact child's device; the service must independently implement the approved authority/consent method before processing real child data. The service is for appropriately authorized parental monitoring, not covert tracking of spouses, adults or third parties.

## 2. Service description

The planned services include some combination of location history, app usage reports, authenticated controls on supported devices, and optionally authorized storage integrations. Available features vary by Android version, handset, permissions, subscription status and device-management enrollment. No uninterrupted location or universal restriction/camera/audio capability is guaranteed.

The parent app and child app may be separate applications. Managed-device provisioning must be described before enrollment, including any need to reset the phone and the effect on device use.
