## Acceptance

> "Exactly 1008 distinct families; child added later eligible"

1. **Distinct Family Allocation:** Qualifying rank is assigned per **family**, strictly between 1 and 1008 (`CHECK(qualifying_rank BETWEEN 1 AND 1008)`). Multiple purchases or multiple children from the same family do not increment the rank.
2. **Boundary Enforcement:** Families 1 through 1008 qualify for founding pricing (₹50/mo or 5,000 paise; ₹500/yr or 50,000 paise). The 1009th distinct family cannot claim founding rank and is billed at the standard public rate (₹99/mo or 9,900 paise; ₹999/yr or 99,900 paise).
3. **Child Inheritance:** When a founding family adds another child later, that child is automatically billed at founding rate because eligibility is keyed to `family_id`.
4. **Addon Behavior:** Extra viewer addon purchases (₹100 once for 100 days) do not qualify an unpaid family for founding rank (`c.kind <> 'ADDON'`).
