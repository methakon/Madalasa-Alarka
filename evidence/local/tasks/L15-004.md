## Acceptance

> "No offline GPS replay after deletion"

1. **Atomic Revocation Before Erasure:**
   - In PostgreSQL, `ap_m1.request_child_erasure` invokes `_purge_child_data` under row-level table locks on `children` and `devices`.
   - All associated devices are transitioned to `enrollment_state = 'REVOKED'`, SPKI public keys are zeroed, and session tokens are revoked (`ap_m1.trusted_sessions.revoked_at`, `ap_m1.device_key_records.state = 'REVOKED'`).
   - Permanent tombstone `ap_m1.child_erasure_tombstones(child_id, 'AUTHORIZED_ERASURE')` is inserted before store deletion proceeds.
2. **Offline GPS Replay Prevention:**
   - Evaluated by `ma::privacy::ErasureReplayGuard`: any offline telemetry batch submitted by an offline device (whether timestamped before, during, or after erasure) is unconditionally rejected fail-closed (`can_ingest_telemetry` evaluates false, and `is_offline_replay_rejected` evaluates true).
   - In SQL, `ap_m1.owns_active_device(...)` returns false for revoked devices, preventing `ap_m1.ingest_location_batch` from committing replayed records.
3. **Suppression of Replay and Restore Reintroduction:**
   - Tombstones prevent backup restore runs from resurrecting erased child records or accepting late deduplication events (`test_21_restore_suppression_reapplies_durable_tombstone`).
