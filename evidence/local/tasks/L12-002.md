## Acceptance

> "Deep links require auth and child grants"

1. **Authentication Guard:** Any unauthenticated caller attempting to resolve or open a deep link (e.g. `madalasa://child/{childId}` or `https://app.madalasa.org/child/{childId}`) is strictly denied with `DeepLinkResolution.DeniedUnauthenticated` and redirected to the authentication flow (`auth`). No child profile, location, or history is requested or exposed.
2. **Child-Specific Grant Guard:** Even if authenticated, navigating to a child identifier requires an active, verified child grant:
   - Unknown child IDs return `DeniedUnauthorizedChild` and redirect to `child_selection`.
   - Non-admitted child profiles (e.g. `PENDING_CONSENT` or `REVOKED`) return `DeniedUnauthorizedChild` and redirect to `child_selection`.
   - Children belonging to a family outside the parent's verified `familyIds` return `DeniedUnauthorizedChild` and redirect to `child_selection`.
3. **Deep Link Ingestion:** `AndroidManifest.xml` registers `VIEW` intent filters for `madalasa://child` and `https://app.madalasa.org/child`. `MainActivity` captures incoming deep link intents via `onCreate` and `onNewIntent`, forwarding them to `MadalasaNavHost` where `ParentDeepLinkNavigator` verifies authorization prior to selecting the child and navigating to `shell`.
4. **Sub-Route Preservation:** Authorized deep links with sub-routes (such as `/location` or `/privacy`) preserve the destination subpage upon authentication and child grant verification.
