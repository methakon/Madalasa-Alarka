## Acceptance

> "No accidental forever retain or silent 14/30 day guess"

Implemented as a fail-closed evaluation: a category enables purge scheduling only when
its decision is `Approved` **and** carries a positive period **and** an approval
reference **and** an effective date. Every other input — undecided, missing period,
zero, negative, missing reference, missing effective date — keeps scheduling disabled and
reports no period. Planning candidates (14/30/90/180/365) are exposed only through
`retention_planning_candidate_days()` so their presence is traceable, and are never
returned as an operative period. The six-hour free visibility window is a separate
query-bound constant and is not a storage lifetime.
