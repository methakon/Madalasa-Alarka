## Acceptance

> "Portrait branding and accessibility pass"

- **Portrait branding:** `drawable-nodpi/ic_launcher_face.png` must exist, be non-trivial
  in size, and carry a valid PNG signature — a renamed text stub fails.
- **Every layer resolves:** the adaptive icon must declare background, foreground and
  monochrome layers, and every drawable it references must resolve to a checked-in file.
- **Density coverage:** mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi and xxxhdpi buckets each ship a PNG, and
  the round variant exists.
- **Accessibility / OS integration:** the monochrome layer is what Android uses for themed
  launcher icons; its presence is asserted, as is `@string/app_name` labelling rather than
  a hard-coded string.
- **Honest child copy:** the child strings must still say monitoring is not enabled and that
  data is synthetic, and must never promise guaranteed or emergency response.
- **English-only initially:** locale-qualified `values-*` directories and non-English
  scripts in strings are rejected.
