## Acceptance

> "Expired paid reverts free with reduced cadence"

`resolve_entitlement(state, premium_addon_held)` maps each entitlement state to an effective
plan. An expired prepaid balance or an ended trial resolves to **exactly** the free
capability set: paid features off, the six-hour visible window, and the documented reduced
cadence. No entry path to the free tier yields a wider window or a faster cadence than any
other — asserted as a single-fingerprint check across `Free`, `PaidExpired` and
`TrialExpired`.

Reduced cadence is expressed with the only two figures the governance sources actually
state:

| Class | Cadence target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Active paid / trial | 1–2 minutes | SRS: eligible JSON batch **upload** target while eligible |
| Free / reverted | 10–15 minutes | SRS: "reduced adaptive GPS interval (moving design target approximately 10–15 minutes…)" |

Paid history depth is **not specified anywhere**, so it is modelled as `std::optional` left
empty rather than guessed, and the test asserts it stays empty for every state. Media stays
off in all states while `FREE_ONLY` is in force.

## Negative / fail-closed cases covered

- Expired plan exposes no paid feature, no paid media, and no unspecified history depth.
- The reduced cadence is asserted to be strictly slower than the eligible target, so an
  "expiry" that silently kept the fast cadence would fail.
- A held premium addon after lapse is retained but **not** active, and a dormant addon never
  counts as an active paid feature.
- The free state cannot acquire paid capability by being resolved.
- All three free-tier entry paths produce one identical capability fingerprint.
