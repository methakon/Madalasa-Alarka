## Acceptance

> "Monitoring never hidden; child-visible notice"

1. **Disclosure-First Setup Flow:** The initial landing destination of the child application setup is the Transparency & Mutual Awareness Disclosure (`ChildNavRoute.Disclosure`), presenting the 4-point Child Privacy & Rights Charter:
   - Exact-Child Pairing: connects exclusively to verified guardians approved for this exact child.
   - No Hidden Surveillance: covert camera, ambient microphone recording, screen scraping, and keylogging strictly prohibited by architecture and code.
   - Visible Protection: prominent, persistent Android system notification visible whenever services are operational.
   - Local Device Checks: hardware and app permission status inspected locally without covert transmission.
2. **Setup Gate Enforcement:** Device pairing cannot proceed without explicit acknowledgment of the disclosure (`ChildPairingPolicy.validate(disclosureAcknowledged = false)` fails with `IllegalStateException("Review and acknowledge monitoring disclosure before pairing")`).
3. **Child-Visible Ongoing Notification Invariant:** Active monitoring requires `MonitoringDisclosure.persistentSpec`:
   - Ongoing (non-dismissible / swiping prevented).
   - Prominently visible while monitoring is active.
   - Clearly identifies the application ("Alarka is active").
   - Explicitly discloses guardian monitoring ("This device is being monitored with guardian consent").
   - Covert, silent, dismissible, or anonymous notification specs are strictly rejected (`isValidDisclosure() == false`), making covert operation impossible by construction.
4. **Google Play Monitoring Tool Declaration:** `AndroidManifest.xml` explicitly declares `<meta-data android:name="isMonitoringTool" android:value="child_monitoring" />` and provides standard, non-hidden launcher activity and icon.
