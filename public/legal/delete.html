Account & Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

What gets deleted

  • Your Google Sign-In identity record and authentication tokens
  • Family and guardian relationships you created
  • Location history and trip records associated with your account
  • Named places, alert preferences and app settings
  • SOS check-in history and notification records
  • Any child device pairings you authorised

What is not deleted by this request

  • Anonymised aggregate statistics that cannot be re-linked to you
  • Records retained for legal compliance obligations (where applicable)
  • Data held by other guardians for their own paired child accounts

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address linked to your Madalasa account. We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

Please include in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Whether you want full account deletion or only specific data removed
Email deletion request
Child-device data: If you are a child-device (Alarka) user, ask your parent or guardian to submit the deletion request from their Madalasa account, or email us directly and we will verify and process the request.
