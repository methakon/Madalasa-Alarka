TRANSPARENT CONSUMER TERMS

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Built on fair prepaid terms, zero forced auto-debits, and a risk-free 48-hour free trial.

Summary of Our Consumer Commitment: • 48-Hour Free Trial for all new paired families with zero billing obligation.
• Transparent prepaid pricing: ₹99/month or ₹999/year per child. No sneaky bank mandates or auto-renewals without notice.
• Full statutory refund options through Google Play and our direct Berhampore grievance desk.

1. Free Trial & Initial Access

When you link a child phone (Alarka) to the parent app (Madalasa), you receive an unconditional 48-hour complimentary trial of premium features (live location breadcrumb trail, safe zone alerts, and priority SOS routing). We do not collect credit card or UPI mandate information to start this trial. If you decide not to purchase a plan, access smoothly transitions to the basic free tier with no penalty or charges.

2. Prepaid Plans & No Forced Auto-Debit

Unlike predatory surveillance apps that charge high recurring fees, Madalasa plans are structured as prepaid validity vouchers:

  • Monthly Child Seat: ₹99 per child for 30 days of active service.
  • Annual Founding Seat: ₹999 per child for 365 days of active service.

When your validity voucher period concludes, service pauses automatically. We will notify you in advance; your bank account or payment method will never be debited automatically without your explicit manual renewal.

3. Subscription Cancellation

If you subscribed via Google Play In-App Billing:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Madalasa and tap Cancel subscription.

Important: Simply uninstalling the app from your phone does not cancel an active subscription or grant a refund. You must cancel through Google Play or contact our support desk.

4. Refund Eligibility & Timelines

We honor refund requests under the following conditions:

  • Within 48 Hours of Purchase: You can request an automated, immediate refund directly through Google Play by visiting Google Play Order History.
  • Technical Malfunction: If technical server issues or verified software bugs prevent the delivery of core safety services, and our technical desk cannot resolve it within 7 business days, a full pro-rated refund will be credited.
  • Accidental / Duplicate Transactions: Double payments caused by payment gateway timeouts are refunded in full upon verification.

Refunds are credited to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit card, or Net Banking) within 5 to 7 business days depending on your banking institution.

5. Account Deletion vs. Plan Cancellation

Canceling a paid plan simply suspends premium GPS features at the end of the paid term. If you wish to permanently erase your entire family profile, child accounts, and stored records, you can submit an irrevocable deletion request via our DPDP Data Erasure Portal.
