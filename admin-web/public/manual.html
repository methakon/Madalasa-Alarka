Official Application Manual

A complete walkthrough for parents and children on installing, pairing, establishing safe geofences, and optimizing battery life.

Madalasa Parent App Walkthrough

Madalasa is installed exclusively on the guardian's smartphone. It manages family membership, geofences, and live GPS map views.

1

Install & Authenticate with Google

Download Madalasa from Google Play. Launch the app and tap Continue with Google. Authenticating with your verified Google account establishes your adult identity safely. No third-party passwords or insecure email tokens are required.

2

Create Your Family Vault

On first login, tap Create Family. Enter your family name and register your child's display name and age. This provisions an isolated PostgreSQL cryptographic tenant partition under strict Row-Level Security (RLS).

3

Generate the 6-Digit Pairing Code

Select your child from the dashboard and tap "Pair Device". Madalasa generates a single-use 6-digit cryptographic code (e.g., 849 201). This code is securely tied to this specific child and expires automatically in 15 minutes.

4

Live Map & Geofence Boundaries

Once paired, view your child's live location on the interactive map. Tap Set Safe Zone to draw geofences around school or home. You will receive notifications whenever your child arrives or leaves.

5

Receiving an Emergency SOS

If your child triggers the SOS button in Alarka, Madalasa sounds an immediate audible alert, zooms directly to their live coordinates, and presents one-tap options to phone the child or direct emergency services.

Alarka Child App Walkthrough

Alarka is installed on the child's smartphone (Android 9.0+). It operates with complete transparency and visible system notifications.

1

Download Alarka onto Child's Phone

Install Alarka from Google Play onto your child's Android phone. Open the app together with your child so they understand how their location is shared.

2

Review Transparent Permissions

Alarka presents an honest permission checklist:
• Location: Set to "Allow all the time" so location works when walking to school.
• Physical Activity: Used to sleep GPS sensors when stationary and conserve battery.
• Notifications: Required for persistent mutual trust shield notification.

3

Enter Parent's 6-Digit Code

Type the 6-digit pairing code generated on the parent's Madalasa app. The two phones establish an authenticated cryptographic handshake. The screen confirms: "Paired with [Parent Name]".

4

The Trust Shield & One-Tap SOS

The home screen displays the child's safety status. In any dangerous situation, holding the prominent red EMERGENCY SOS button for 2 seconds immediately alerts guardians with high-precision location updates.

Android Battery & Background Optimization

To ensure uninterrupted location protection when the child's phone screen is turned off, apply these settings according to your phone brand:

A

Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)

1. Open Settings → Apps → Manage Apps → Alarka.
2. Enable Autostart.
3. Tap Battery Saver and select "No restrictions".
4. Open Recent Apps screen, long-press Alarka, and tap the Lock icon to prevent clearing from memory.

B

Samsung Galaxy (OneUI)

1. Open Settings → Battery and device care → Battery.
2. Tap Background usage limits.
3. Check Never sleeping apps and tap + to add Alarka.
4. Ensure Adaptive battery is not throttling Alarka.

C

OnePlus / Oppo / Realme (OxygenOS / ColorOS)

1. Open Settings → Battery → More battery settings → Optimize battery use.
2. Find Alarka and set to "Don't optimize".
3. In App Management → Alarka, enable Allow background activity and Allow auto launch.
