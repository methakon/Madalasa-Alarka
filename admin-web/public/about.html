OUR ORIGINS & VALUES

Wisdom Over Surveillance

We believe that raising confident, safe children in the digital age requires open conversation and transparent technology—never clandestine spyware.

ANCIENT INDIAN HERITAGE

The Lullaby of Queen Madalasa

In the ancient Markandeya Purana, Queen Madalasa, consort of King Ritadhvaja (Kuvalayasva), chose a unique method to nurture her children. Instead of reciting tales of fear, demons, or possessive attachment, she sang lullabies of pure philosophical wisdom—awakening them to self-sovereignty, moral clarity, and non-violence.

When her fourth son, Prince Alarka, was destined to become king, Madalasa imparted to him the sacred royal duties (Raja Dharma): how to protect the realm with vigilance without succumbing to tyranny or paranoia.

Our project takes its name and philosophy from this timeless dialogue: parents who guide with protective love, and children who grow in open self-governance.

The Madalasa Upakhyana

"A guardian who resorts to deceit and silent monitoring admits failure of moral authority. True protection is shared in the light of day."

ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

How We Build For Safety

World-class cryptographic and systems engineering designed to keep your family safe.

C++20 Drogon Core

Ultra high-throughput, non-blocking asynchronous backend engineered in modern C++20. Consumes under 15MB RAM on our cloud servers, preventing bloat, latency, and security surface vulnerabilities.

Encrypted WebRTC P2P

Direct authenticated peer-to-peer live location streaming between parent and child devices over ICE/WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP encryption. Coordinates flow directly without intermediate archival.

DPDP Act 2023 Built-In

Engineered strictly under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Explicit parental consent verification, immediate audit trail, and unconditional, one-click right to data erasure.

ROOTED IN BENGAL

Proudly Engineered in Berhampore

Madalasa–Alarka is conceived and maintained in historical Berhampore, Murshidabad, West Bengal. Our servers are co-located in India's sovereign data zones (Hyderabad Oracle A1 cloud facility) ensuring that Indian family location records never traverse foreign surveillance borders.
