1. Getting Started & Pairing

Install Madalasa on the parent's phone and Alarka on the child's phone. In the parent app, navigate to My Children → Add Child to generate a secure, 6-digit cryptographic pairing code (valid for 15 minutes). On the child's phone, open Alarka, accept the transparent permission disclosure, and type that 6-digit code. Pairing completes instantly.

2. Privacy, Child Dignity & DPDP Act

3. Location Reliability & Battery

Aggressive manufacturer power management (such as Xiaomi MIUI Battery Saver, Samsung Deep Sleeping Apps, or Vivo Background Freezing) often kills background services. To fix this, open Android Settings → Apps → Alarka → Battery, and select "Unrestricted" (or disable battery optimization).
